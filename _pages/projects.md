### [NHL Game Outcome Ensemble Modeling](/nhl-predictions/)
Built and compared GLM, Random Forest, XGBoost, Neural Network, and ensemble models for predicting NHL game outcomes using bookmaker odds and engineered features.

### [Hip Hypermobility Statistical Analysis](/hip-hypermobility/)
Applied regression and multiple imputation to clinical data to assess the relationship between hip hypermobility and psychosocial measures in a hospital cohort.

Developed an ensemble modeling framework in R to predict taxi ride cancellations, using SMOTE and prioritizing specificity for operational improvements.
